# ZM-18 — Trends and emerging skills

## Zielmodus instruction

Implement a separate **Trends & emerging skills** workbench that connects authoritative external signals to DEKRA's Mobility, Digital Trust and Sustainability strategy and to its value-generating activities. Compare qualified signals with the governed skill catalogue and immutable job-role evidence to identify emerging technical, functional and behavioural skill candidates.

The capability shall:

1. maintain an allowlisted, dated source register and run quarterly plus on material events;
2. show a time-horizon radar and a trend-to-value-chain heatmap;
3. calculate a transparent priority index from strategic fit, urgency, value impact, role breadth and catalogue gap;
4. distinguish covered concepts, partial matches and white spots;
5. show affected job families and evidence-linked stored job descriptions without changing source descriptions;
6. prepare suggestion-only candidate packages with definition, category, expected level and traceable sources;
7. require functional-SME validation, HR/taxonomy review and governance-board approval before catalogue inclusion;
8. keep approval, publishing and edits to approved skills outside agent authority;
9. prepare future integration with SAP SuccessFactors as an enrichment source, not as the current system of record;
10. pass lint, type-check, production build and browser UAT before publication.

## Acceptance criteria

- The new tab is directly accessible from Skill Designer navigation.
- Radar, heatmap, white-spot queue, role impact and source/method views are responsive and keyboard accessible.
- Group 1 reflects the supplied 285-role catalogue across its six job families; Groups 2 and 3 are represented as the agreed future scope.
- Candidates are clearly labelled as proposals and cannot be approved or published from the radar.
- Source URLs and the scoring weights are visible to reviewers.
- Documentation and machine-readable configuration are separate from the user interface.
