# ZM-13 — Evidence-bound MECE job-to-skill design

## Target outcome

Job architects and taxonomy stewards can convert target job descriptions into
an explainable role profile of normally eight to ten distinct skills. The
profile is mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive across material
responsibility and outcome evidence, uses approved Korn Ferry-aligned catalog
skills first, and creates a governed new-skill draft only when no acceptable
approved concept exists.

## Authoritative content boundary

- The agent may use configured Korn Ferry metadata, public-safe framework
  references and internally authorized interpretations.
- Licensed definitions are incorporated only after an authorized source and
  access-controlled administration path are supplied.
- Licensed definitions, credentials and protected references never enter the
  public GitHub Pages bundle.

## Method

1. Ingest target job-description evidence and preserve file/location lineage.
2. Normalize responsibilities, outcomes, activities, tools, qualifications,
   context and constraints.
3. Resolve critical contradictions and reach at least 80% evidence sufficiency.
4. Match approved canonical skills before considering new concepts.
5. Compose an eight-to-ten-skill profile with one primary skill owner for every
   material responsibility/outcome and exactly 100% total weight.
6. Route uncovered evidence to clarification or a governed taxonomy-gap draft.
7. Expose direct evidence, thirteen score dimensions, rejected alternatives,
   omissions and evidence-adjusted confidence intervals.
8. Stop at named human review. The agent cannot approve or publish.

## Acceptance criteria

- The in-app **Mapping playbook** explains the method, click path, decision
  logic, worked example and troubleshooting.
- **Jobs & mapping** displays the eight-to-ten target, MECE coverage, overlap,
  uncovered evidence, profile weight and profile confidence interval.
- A mapping batch is review-ready only when all material evidence has exactly
  one primary owner, mappings use distinct approved skills and weights total
  100%.
- A new-skill proposal includes Action + Object + Outcome syntax, evidence
  references, boundary, observability, duplicate analysis and confidence.
- Reviewers can enter their identity and rationale once and apply decisions
  without repeated browser prompts. Batch approval remains disabled until the
  quality gate passes.
- UAT exercises the existing synthetic intake documents and stops before any
  approval or publication action.
